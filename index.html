OrriGo Mail

InboxGo – a small Gmail app for Windows, made by OrriGo.

InboxGo shows your Gmail inbox in a fast desktop window with a tray icon and notifications. It is a personal project, shared with friends and family.

  • Runs on your own Windows PC (also from a USB stick).
  • You sign in with your own Google account.
  • Your mail goes straight between your PC and Google. There is no OrriGo server.
