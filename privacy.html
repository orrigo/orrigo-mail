Privacy policy

InboxGo by OrriGo · last updated 1 November 2026

What the app does with your Google data

InboxGo is a desktop program for Windows. When you sign in, Google lets it read and organise your Gmail (read, label, archive, delete, send and draft mail) and, if you allow it, show your contacts' photos. All of this happens on your own PC, only to show and manage your mail for you.

Where your data is kept

  • Your mail is downloaded from Google straight to your PC and kept only in the InboxGo folder on that PC.
  • The sign-in is stored encrypted in the same folder. Deleting the folder removes everything.
  • OrriGo has no server. Your mail, contacts and sign-in are never sent to OrriGo or to anyone else.
  • Your data is not sold, not used for ads, and not shared.

Optional features that talk to other services

  • Cloud AI (off unless you switch it on): if you choose a cloud AI service in Settings and add your own key, the text of the mail you ask it to summarise, translate or answer is sent to that service. A local AI on your PC sends nothing out.
  • Sender logos: the program may ask a public logo service for the picture of a sender's web domain (only the domain name, never the mail).

Google API Services

InboxGo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in InboxGo (Settings → Account) or remove its access at any time on myaccount.google.com/permissions.
